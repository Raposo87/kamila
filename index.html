Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide legal advice?

No. I provide HR and people-process support. Legal, tax, payroll, and accounting questions are handled together with trusted local specialists, and I coordinate with them for you.

Do I need a Polish entity?

Not necessarily. Some companies hire through an EOR, others establish their own entity. In the introductory call we can outline the options, and your legal advisor can confirm the right structure.

How quickly can we start?

After the introductory call, we agree the scope and I can usually start within a few weeks, depending on current availability.

Can you work alongside an EOR or payroll provider?

Yes. This is a common setup: the EOR or payroll provider handles employment and payroll, and I handle the HR processes around it.
